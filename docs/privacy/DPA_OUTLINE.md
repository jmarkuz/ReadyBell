# Data processing agreement: outline for legal review

Between the restaurant (controller) and ReadyBell (processor). Not a contract.

1. **Subject, nature and duration**: order tracking and push notification for the restaurant's customers; for the term of the service.
2. **Data and subjects**: customers (optional name, ordered items, random link, push subscription, consent record); the restaurant's staff
   (username, password hash, role). See [RECORDS_OF_PROCESSING.md](RECORDS_OF_PROCESSING.md).
3. **Instructions**: the processor acts on the controller's documented instructions; the settings the restaurant makes in the service
   (for example the retention period for customer names within the allowed range) are instructions.
4. **Confidentiality**: persons authorised to process are bound to confidentiality.
5. **Security**: the measures in the records of processing; changes will not lower the level.
6. **Sub-processors**: the list (hosting, push services) with a way to object to changes; the processor flows the same obligations down.
7. **Transfers outside the EU/EEA**: the mechanism (standard contractual clauses) for push services and hosting.
8. **Help with data subject rights**: the service gives customers self-service download and erasure; the restaurant admin tools find and erase
   by order number; the processor answers within [x] days when asked.
9. **Personal data breach**: the processor notifies the controller without undue delay and within [24-48] hours; the runbook is in
   [RETENTION_AND_BACKUPS.md](RETENTION_AND_BACKUPS.md).
10. **End of the service**: the controller gets an export (orders, staff, settings) for [30] days, then all data of the restaurant is deleted
    within [90] days (backups expire within their retention); anonymous facts may be kept if the contract says so.
11. **Audits**: information on request and an audit once a year at the controller's cost.
12. **Liability and governing law**: [to be agreed].
